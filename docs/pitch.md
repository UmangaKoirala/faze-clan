# FaZe Draft
Scout the next gaming star.

## The problem
Promising gaming creators are hard to discover.
Fans who spot talent early have no way to track their scouting success.

## Our solution
Draft five rising creators with a $50 fantasy budget.
Earn points from their measured YouTube growth.
See community favorites on a talent watchlist.

## Who benefits
- Fans compete to discover talent early.
- Emerging creators gain visibility.
- Talent teams discover creators worth a closer look.
